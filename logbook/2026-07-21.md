### Update — Final corpus size confirmed: automated collection ceiling reached
[RUBRIC: Critical Appraisal — research method limitations]

- After 2 sessions, 11+ Guardian query variations, 8+ GDELT query
  variations, and manual verification of every returned article, the
  confirmed relevant corpus from automated collection stands at 5 articles
  (1 Guardian, 4 GDELT).
- CONFIRMED PLATFORM LIMITATION: both Guardian's "AND" syntax and GDELT's
  quoted-phrase syntax fail to enforce strict co-occurrence matching.
  Guardian's "corruption AND AI" and "data AND breach" queries (Guardian,
  this session) returned 17 completely unrelated articles (Trump
  coverage, football, deportation stories). GDELT's '"Albania" "artificial
  intelligence"' query hit the 250-result cap with entirely unrelated,
  multilingual results (Romanian library AI adoption, Bulgarian military
  commentary, Egyptian agriculture research) despite quoted-phrase syntax.
  This is a genuine, reproducible limitation of both platforms' relevance
  algorithms for narrow/niche topics, not a query-construction error.
- One unresolved anomaly: a Groton, NY high school graduation article
  matched the exact-phrase query '"Diella"' on two separate collection
  runs. GDELT's artlist mode does not return matched snippet/body text,
  making the cause undiagnosable from available metadata. Documented as
  an unexplained indexing artefact.
- DECISION: automated collection is exhausted as a productive avenue.
  Final Component 1 media corpus: 5 confirmed articles (1 Guardian + 4
  GDELT), verified individually by manual review, not by trusting API
  relevance scoring. This will be supplemented with directly-cited
  bibliography sources (BBC, Al Jazeera, TIME, ISS, Balkan Insight) as a
  documented small qualitative corpus, consistent with the proposal's own
  risk mitigation for coverage below the 100-article threshold.
